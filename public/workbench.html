What the current workbench measures

Learn from observations

Three completed calibration runs expose a system's behavior. The actor infers its hidden delay and response to repeated inputs. The provided-rule control supplies the mechanism directly.

Predict fresh outcomes

Six categorical predictions concern one fresh run. Calibration is not scored. Prediction accuracy and all-query success are scored by the simulator; future outcomes stay hidden until the attempt finishes.

Keep comparisons explicit

Matched conditions vary timing and mechanism access. The foundational temporal suite retains historical-state queries and optional fresh retry, trace memory, reflection and pincer recovery.

This first dynamics release has one public development template and permits one unassisted attempt. It does not yet evaluate pincer or reflection as dynamics-learning strategies, probabilistic forecasts, real-world transfer or checkable past explanations. Past diagnosis remains a secondary project diagnostic. Reference scores verify the harness, not model ability. All suites are experimental; no novelty, model ranking or strategy advantage is assumed.
